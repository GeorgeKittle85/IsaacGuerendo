FlightGear Web

The JSBSim flight model compiled to WebAssembly, over FlightGear's own scenery of the San Francisco Bay Area and Oregon.

Scenery layers

Cessna, 747-400, scenery, ground networks and systems: FlightGear (GPL). 737 MAX 8: the 737-family project (GPL). Flight model and F-16 aerodynamics: JSBSim (LGPL/GPL). Taxiway names: X-Plane Scenery Gateway (GPL). Buildings, roads and railways: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Aerial imagery: USDA NAIP via the USGS National Map (public domain). Rendering: three.js.

Keys

FlightGear's default keyboard bindings. Click switches and knobs in the cockpit; the mouse wheel turns knobs. Gamepads and joysticks work too.
