FlightGear Web

FlightGear's Cessna 172P, flown by the JSBSim flight model compiled to WebAssembly, over FlightGear's own San Francisco Bay Area scenery.

Aircraft, scenery and systems: FlightGear (GPL). Flight model: JSBSim (LGPL). Rendering: three.js.

Keys

FlightGear's default keyboard bindings. Click switches and knobs in the cockpit; the mouse wheel turns knobs. Gamepads and joysticks work too.
